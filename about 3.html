Our Story

Food, Made With Intention

Yumly Yours started with a simple question: why should eating well cost you your time? Here's how we're answering it.

How We Started

Built for people with no time to waste

Yumly Yours began in Victoria Island with one goal: give busy Lagosians restaurant-quality meals without the hours spent shopping, prepping, and cooking. What started as a small delivery kitchen has grown into a full dining ecosystem — restaurant, subscriptions, private chefs, and corporate catering, all from one trusted kitchen.


Every dish still goes through the same standard our first customers tasted: fresh ingredients, real Nigerian and continental flavor, and food that's ready exactly when you need it.

🎯

Our Mission

To give busy professionals, families, and teams back their time — without asking them to compromise on the quality of what they eat.

🌍

Our Vision

To become West Africa's most trusted name in premium meal solutions — from the family dinner table to the boardroom.
